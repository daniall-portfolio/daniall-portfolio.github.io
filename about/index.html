Story

I started with event posters. The questions kept getting bigger.

I have worked in design since 2019. I began in high school, designing posters and visual identities for events, then continued with other event work and freelance projects while studying Computer Engineering.

Since then, my work has moved across brand identities, campaigns, social-media systems, event communication, UI, illustration, presentation design, EdTech, and both national and international events. I have worked independently, as an in-house designer, as a team member, and as a graphic team lead.

The engineering background still matters. It shapes how I organize information, test systems, and think through how one visual decision affects everything that follows. Outside conventional design work, I have designed puzzles and built mechanical escape-room puzzles with Arduino.

I’m especially drawn to creative projects that leave room for exploration, experimentation, and unexpected ideas.
