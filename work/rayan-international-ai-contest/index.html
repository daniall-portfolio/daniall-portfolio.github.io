Rayan International AI Contest

Extending an existing blue–green AI identity across participant, social, email, information, and environmental touchpoints.

Role

Overview

Rayan International AI Contest was an international trustworthy-AI programming competition with a $35,000 prize pool and an in-person presentation stage for top teams.

  • Event Communication Design
  • Participant Identity System
  • Social Media Templates
  • LinkedIn Content
  • Email Marketing
  • Information Design
  • Environmental Graphics
  • Campaign Exploration
01 · Context

The brief

The logo, palette, and blue–green visual system already existed. The task was to translate that language into new digital, print, participant, and environmental applications without treating the work as an identity redesign.

02 · Approach

The idea

Working within Rayan’s existing visual identity, I translated its blue–green color palette, technical linework, geometric structures, and typography into a consistent range of digital, print, and environmental applications. Rather than redesigning the identity, the focus was on extending it across new formats while preserving clarity and visual continuity.

03 · Contribution

My role

I worked as a graphic team member and personally designed every supplied asset shown here. Both event-wall designs were produced and used. The Trustworthiness in Deep Learning poster appears only as an unused direction.
