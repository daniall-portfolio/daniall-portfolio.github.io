Case study 03 / 08 — In-house Brand Project

LingoEducatia

A warm, supportive language-education identity built from an existing umbrella mark into a complete sub-brand system.

Client / context
Language-education sub-brand of LingoPotentia
Year
2025–Present
Role
Brand & Graphic Designer
Status
Ongoing in-house brand development

Overview

LingoPotentia was developed into an umbrella brand, with LingoEducatia as its first language-education sub-brand. I refined the existing umbrella mark slightly for this context, then designed the identity, guidance, campaigns, and applications from the ground up while contributing to strategy.

  • Sub-brand Development
  • Logo Refinement
  • Brand Strategy Collaboration
  • Visual Identity
  • Brand Guidelines
  • Campaign Design
  • Digital & Print Applications
01 · Context

The brief

The visual system needed to serve learners across age groups, languages, physical classrooms, digital products, campaigns, certificates, and internal communications without losing clarity or warmth.

02 · Approach

The idea

The identity combines a speech bubble, an open hand, and a layered book. A blue–green core communicates trust and growth, while coral, yellow, mint, and lavender create a flexible educational palette. Omnes and Farhang support a clear bilingual hierarchy.

03 · Contribution

My role

The umbrella logo existed before this project. I refined it slightly for LingoEducatia and independently designed all supplied visual assets, guidelines, applications, campaigns, and the internal character system. I also participated in strategic development.
