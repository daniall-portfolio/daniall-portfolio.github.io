Case study 07 / 08 — Freelance Client Project

HasTea

An evolving character-led identity that transforms an existing café logo into a warm, humorous, illustrated world centered on its owner.

Client / context
Iranian café in Mumbai
Year
2025–Present · Redesign 2026
Role
Visual Identity Designer, Illustrator & Copywriter
Status
Printed and in use

Overview

An evolving character-led identity for an Iranian café in Mumbai, transforming its existing logo into a warm and playful illustrated world centered around the café’s owner. The character Hasti, designed to represent the owner, became the system's central voice.

  • Visual Identity Development
  • Character Design
  • Illustration
  • Menu Design
  • Loyalty Program
  • Brand Copywriting
  • Printed Customer Touchpoints
01 · Context

The brief

The original logo existed before I joined. I first built a warm paper-textured visual system around it; later, the client felt that system had become repetitive and requested a more expressive identity and redesigned menu.

02 · Approach

The idea

The 2026 redesign introduces a bolder Hasti character, darker green, expressive café scenes, customer and food illustrations, pointing hands, a cat, stronger editorial hierarchy, and more confident, humorous copywriting. All supplied applications were printed and used.

03 · Contribution

My role

I developed and redesigned the visual identity around the existing logo, designed Hasti and the illustration world, created the menu and loyalty touchpoints, and wrote headings including “Your Comfort Zone,” “Tea Time? Anytime.,” “Vegan Milk Line,” and “Only at HasTea.”
