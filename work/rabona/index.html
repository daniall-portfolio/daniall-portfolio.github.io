Case study 02 / 08 — Game club identity & tournament campaign

Rabona

A warm, playful identity for a game club: a one-line controller mark, a character, a nine-frame launch grid and the FC 27 cup campaign.

Client / context
Game club (gamenet) — Rabona Cup, FC 27
Year
2026
Role
Visual Identity Designer & Illustrator
Status
2026 — Rabona Cup FC 27 upcoming

Overview

Rabona is a game club built around playing together. Its visual identity balances the ease of a friendly get-together with the excitement of the game, and its first big moment is the Rabona Cup, an FC 27 tournament.

  • Visual Identity
  • Brand Presentation
  • Character Design
  • Iconography
  • Instagram Launch Grid
  • Highlight Stories
  • Tournament Campaign
01 · Context

The brief

The club needed an identity that felt friendly rather than aggressive, worked in Persian first, and could carry both everyday information — hours, contact, route — and a tournament campaign.

02 · Approach

The idea

Bold orange on warm black and cream, soft rounded forms, and one graphic device — a single rounded line ending in an orange dot, taken from the controller mark. A capsule-bodied character with no mouth expresses everything through posture.

03 · Contribution

My role

I designed the visual identity system and brand presentation, the character and its poses, the icon and highlight-cover set, the nine-post launch illustration, the highlight stories, and the Rabona Cup and student campaigns. The existing logo was rebuilt as clean vectors.

Ch. 01Brand personality

Playing, together.

Rabona brings the feel of a friendly get-together to the excitement of the game. The identity rests on the same balance: bold colour, soft forms, and restrained detail.

Ch. 02The mark

A controller drawn in one line.

The mark is a game controller traced as a single rounded contour, set above a light, wide wordmark. It is drawn on here the way it is built — line first, then the buttons.

01 · Black on orange

Ch. 03Colour & type

Distinct colours, readable type.

Cream for clarity, black for depth, orange for emphasis. Gruppo sets the wide Latin wordmark; Dana carries all Persian text. Click a colour to copy it.

Ch. 04Line & dot

One stroke, one orange dot.

The controller’s rounded contour leaves the logo and becomes the system’s graphic device: frames, routes and dividers, always ending in a single orange dot.

A player from the world of the game.

A rounded capsule body, orange shoulder joints and no mouth: personality comes from posture alone.
