Case study 08 / 08 — Social-media visual system

Chime

A proposed nine-post Instagram launch translating supplied clinical and human-centered content into an approachable visual narrative.

Client / context
UK music-therapy and wellbeing organization
Year
2025
Role
Social Media Visual Designer & Illustrator
Status
Delivered to Client — Publication Unconfirmed

Overview

A nine-post Instagram launch system designed for a UK music-therapy organization, translating supplied clinical, educational, and human-centered content into an approachable visual narrative.

  • Visual System Design
  • Instagram Grid Design
  • Carousel Design
  • Illustration
  • Iconography
  • Information Design
  • Visual Storytelling
01 · Context

The brief

Chime works across music, mental health, therapy, research, and wellbeing. Its existing logo and client-supplied copy needed a cohesive launch system able to carry human stories, educational information, myths, session explanations, and annual statistics.

02 · Approach

The idea

A calm blue system combines rounded information graphics, intimate photography, simple illustration, and generous spacing. Carousel structures keep clinical information clear while preserving warmth and visual continuity across the nine-post grid.

03 · Contribution

My role

Working with the client’s existing logo and supplied written content, I independently designed the complete visual system and all graphic executions for the proposed nine-post Instagram launch. The project was delivered; publication is unconfirmed.

Ch. 01The idea

A launch that sounds like the name.

“A chime is a sound that resonates. It’s delicate yet powerful, just like the process of healing.”

From the client-supplied launch copy.

The logo’s overlapping discs became the system’s rhythm: they open the welcome post, repeat as soft background rings, and shrink into the little progress marker at the foot of every slide.

Rounded information graphics keep clinical content clear; intimate photography and simple illustration keep it human.

  • 9 posts planned as one grid
  • 29 carousel slides
  • 1 calm blue system

Ch. 02The grid

Nine posts, published in order.

Scroll to publish the launch one post at a time, the way the grid was planned. Tap any published post to open it — carousels swipe.
