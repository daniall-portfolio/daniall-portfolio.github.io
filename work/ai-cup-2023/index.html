AI Cup 2023

A space-themed visual system and campaign for a nationwide AI programming competition, developed while leading a nine-person graphic team.

Client / context
Computer Engineering Scientific Association, Isfahan University of Technology
Year
2023
Role
Graphic Team Lead — Led a nine-person graphic design team and personally designed the main event poster.
Status
Completed event

Overview

AI Cup 2023 invited teams to build AI strategies for a competitive space-themed game using languages including Python, Java, and C++. The project expanded an existing event mark into a broad communication system.

  • Team Leadership
  • Visual Direction
  • Main Event Poster
  • Team Identity System
  • Editorial Design
  • Campaign Assets
  • UI Design Supervision
01 · Context

The brief

The event needed one coherent visual direction across proposals, guides, social campaigns, speakers, prizes, sponsors, team identities, certificates, and a participant dashboard.

02 · Approach

The idea

A dark cosmic field, high-contrast typography, orbital imagery, and a color-coded team icon system created a recognizable but flexible language. The editorial pages prioritize information while retaining the event's spatial atmosphere.

03 · Contribution

My role

I led the nine-person graphic team, directing and reviewing the campaign, editorial, team identity and web UI work shown here. The main event poster is my own design; everything else was designed by team members under my lead. The main logo pre-existed and received a subtle 2023 adjustment.
