Gamein 2022

Year
Held in 2023
Role
Graphic Team Member / Visual Communication Designer
Status
Completed event materials

Overview

A collection of social media and printed communication materials designed for Gamein, a large-scale business simulation event organized by Sharif University of Technology. The sixth edition was called Gamein 2022 and was held in May/June 2023.

  • Social Media Design
  • Carousel Design
  • Information Design
  • Editorial Design
  • Printed Collateral
  • Event Stationery
  • Bilingual Communication
01 · Context

The brief

The existing red–purple identity needed to support recruitment, business-simulation education, participant storytelling, playful cultural references, statistics, stationery, and event collateral in Persian and English.

02 · Approach

The idea

I used the existing hourglass-like mark and bold red forms as a modular editorial frame. Three carousel systems organize the most narrative content: team recruitment, business simulation games, and the Gamein × Squid Game series.

03 · Contribution

My role

The logo and identity existed before my contribution. I personally designed every supplied output shown here. Movie and television imagery embedded in the social designs is contextual source material; my contribution is the visual communication and carousel design. The notebook and certificate were printed and used.

Ch. 01Carousels

Three series, built to be swiped.

Recruitment, business-simulation education and a pop-culture series — each a carousel with its own rhythm. Swipe them on the phones.

Movie and television imagery in the Squid Game series is contextual source material; the contribution is the visual communication and carousel design.
