Proof 03 — Process

How I art-direct a project.

Five steps I take on every brief, each linked to a project where it made the difference.

  1. 01

    Read the brief

    Before any sketch, I turn what the client needs into one communication problem worth solving — and decide what must not change.

    Seen in Rayan International AI ContestExtend the existing identity, don’t redesign it.
  2. 02

    Find the idea

    One concept that makes every later decision easier to judge. If an option doesn’t serve the idea, it doesn’t make the cut.

    Seen in HasTeaThe owner becomes Hasti — the brand’s voice.
  3. 03

    Build the system

    Type, colour, grid, illustration and rules written down, so the identity holds up across every touchpoint and every designer who uses it.

    Seen in LingoEducatiaAn umbrella mark grown into a full sub-brand guide.
  4. 04

    Lead the production

    Brief the team, review every output, and keep quality consistent from the key visual to the last social post.

    Seen in AI Cup 2023Led a nine-person graphic team.
  5. 05

    Present the thinking

    A proposal is a story: why this direction, how it behaves live, and what it asks of the client next.

    Seen in ChimeA nine-post launch, presented as one system.
